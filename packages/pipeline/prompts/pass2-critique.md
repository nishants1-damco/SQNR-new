You review a draft reconstruction of a room, made by another analyst from the same photos and measurements you receive, together with a list of consistency problems the pipeline detected automatically. Return the complete corrected reconstruction, and list what you changed and why in revision_notes as short strings. Most rooms are conference and meeting rooms in offices around the world, so don't assume any one country's building standards.

Work on the shell and portals first, because every object position depends on them.

The shell. Re-derive width_m, length_m and height_m independently of the draft: for each axis, a repeating ceiling or floor module and a known object (typical sizes under REFERENCE SIZES); for the height, the gap above a door head. If your figures differ from the draft's by more than about 10%, use yours and rescale objects and portals with them. If the draft's scale anchor is weak or misidentified, choose a better one and rescale the whole model consistently. Rebuild wall_evidence from all frames: track each physical wall by its corners and ordered landmarks before naming it, keep features that share one uninterrupted wall plane on the same wall, and give opposite walls the same run class. If the draft splits a continuous wall across compass walls, fix the mapping and move its portals and objects with it. A glass partition is a wall, not a window.

Problems. Fix every listed problem and any others you find: objects outside the room, duplicate entries for one item seen in overlapping frames, portals that don't fit their wall or overlap each other, areas inconsistent with the dimensions, a ceiling height outside about 2.2-4.5 m without visible evidence, and absorption values that contradict the measured RT60.

Portals. Check each wall for missed doors, openings and windows, including doors in glass partitions and a side of the room left entirely open (kind "open_side"); add anything the draft omitted, with honest confidence. Kinds: door, doorway, window, archway, opening, open_side, sliding_door, pass_through. offset_m runs from the west end on the north and south walls and from the south end on the east and west walls, to the near edge of the opening; every portal fits within its wall without overlapping another, and sill_m is set on every portal (0 for doors and openings).

Objects. Rebuild the object list wall by wall from the frames, not from the draft. Remove items you can't actually see and small loose clutter; keep every device listed under DEVICE SCOPE. Keep one entry per physical item, counting identical chairs individually, and list each component of a grouping separately (a display, the video bar under it and the credenza below). Frames about 180° apart from one viewpoint face opposite walls, so their objects belong on opposite walls. Items against a wall keep against_wall, yaw_deg and wall_offset_m, and appear in the order of that wall's landmarks_in_order; free-standing items such as the meeting table and its chairs use "none". Preserve nested arrangements with relative_to, spatial_relation and floor_elevation_m, so a chair stays tucked at the table and a display stays mounted above its credenza. Every object includes supporting_headings_deg.

Acoustic cross-checks are rough; where they disagree with clear imagery, follow the imagery. Lower any confidence the evidence doesn't support.
